# Data attribution

HMS-SurgVTG uses **CholecT50**, provided by CAMMA, ICube, University of Strasbourg, France, and its clinical collaborators. The source annotations are adapted into natural-language temporal grounding annotations.

Source: [CholecT50](https://github.com/CAMMA-public/cholect50).

The data are distributed under **Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International**. The source license text is included in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Please cite the source dataset publication:

> C. I. Nwoye, T. Yu, C. Gonzalez, B. Seeliger, P. Mascagni, D. Mutter, J. Marescaux, and N. Padoy. Rendezvous: Attention Mechanisms for the Recognition of Surgical Action Triplets in Endoscopic Videos. Medical Image Analysis, 78 (2022), 102433.
